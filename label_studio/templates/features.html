Features

AI-powered algorithms automatically identify and count cells in your images with high accuracy, reducing manual counting time by up to 90%. Supports various cell types and staining methods.

Detect and analyze particles of various sizes with advanced image processing techniques, providing detailed statistics and measurements. Ideal for quality control and research applications.
